minecraff

Left half of the keyboard: A/D to move, W to jump, S to duck/dig, F or E to act

Right half of the keyboard: Arrow keys to move, Up to jump, Down to duck/dig, / or . to act

Swap seats: press Backspace to flip which half drives Steve and which drives Alex. Alex starts on the left, Steve on the right; each HP bar shows its player's current side

Act: your action key attacks a mob in range, otherwise chops a nearby tree, otherwise digs. Hold your down key with it to dig regardless; add left/right to tunnel sideways

Placement: Press 'P' to open material palette, then press 1-9 to select, click to place, right-click to destroy

Panels: 'I' inventory, 'M' minimap, 'T' tech tree / workshop

Debug: Press 'B' for bounding boxes; with debug on, hold Shift + arrows/WASD to pan the camera
